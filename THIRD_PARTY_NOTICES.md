# Map data and libraries

- Country representative coordinates and numeric codes in `src/country-locations.json` are a subset of [mledoze/countries](https://github.com/mledoze/countries), under the Open Database License (ODbL). This derived dataset is available under ODbL; the license is included in `public/licenses/countries-ODbL.txt`. Coordinates represent countries, not the author's actual visited places. Borders and names are illustrative, not legal guidance.
- World geometries: [world-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas), derived from Natural Earth public-domain cartographic data. World Atlas copyright 2013–2019 Michael Bostock, ISC license (see package license).
- Leaflet copyright 2010–2023 Volodymyr Agafonkin and contributors, BSD-2-Clause license (see package license).
- topojson-client copyright 2012–2019 Michael Bostock, ISC license (see package license).

- 3D globe: [globe.gl](https://github.com/vasturiano/globe.gl), three-globe and Three.js, MIT licenses (see installed package licenses).
- Earth photographic texture: `public/images/earth-blue-marble.jpg`, distributed in the [three-globe examples](https://github.com/vasturiano/three-globe/tree/master/example/img), NASA Blue Marble imagery. This is a static illustration of Earth's surface, not live satellite imagery. See [NASA Visible Earth](https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/collection/1484/blue-marble).

- Detailed Vietnam outline: `src/vietnam-boundary.json`, extracted from Natural Earth Vector `ne_10m_admin_0_countries.geojson` (public domain, Natural Earth 1:10m). This is a country outline, not a current province-level administrative map. The separately drawn city labels are reference locations and are not visited-place records.

- Province layers `public/maps/vietnam-provinces-63.geojson` and `vietnam-provinces-34.geojson`: derived from [Natural Earth Vector ADM1 1:10m](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson), public domain. The 63-unit layer is historical; the 34-unit illustration dissolves those polygons using the 2025 provincial merger groups (Resolution 202/2025/QH15), with Thừa Thiên Huế renamed Huế. This is an illustrative derivative of older geometry, not an authoritative current administrative survey. Province names attached to source ISO codes VN-39, VN-53 and VN-66 are corrected to Đồng Nai, Bắc Kạn and Hưng Yên. Author-supplied visited places retain their original names.

- International regional overlays in `public/maps/international-regions.geojson`: eleven regions with confirmed author destinations, using ADM1 geometries derived from [Natural Earth Vector 1:10m ADM1](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson), public domain, simplified for display. These source boundaries are illustrative and may precede administrative changes; they are not an authoritative current survey. Yunnan's provincial polygon is replaced with only the three visited prefecture-level geometries from [ECharts 3.8.5 Yunnan map](https://github.com/apache/echarts/blob/3.8.5/map/json/province/yunnan.json), decoded from ECharts UTF8 encoding and simplified. ECharts copyright (c) 2017 Baidu Inc., BSD-3-Clause, retained in [public/licenses/echarts-3.8.5-BSD-3-Clause.txt](public/licenses/echarts-3.8.5-BSD-3-Clause.txt). Regional colors distinguish areas; a highlight means a confirmed destination exists within that region, not that the entire region was toured. Singapore uses a full national outline; Fuji is the city confirmed by the author, rather than Mount Fuji.

- Singapore national outline: [georgique/world-geojson, countries/singapore.json](https://github.com/georgique/world-geojson/blob/develop/countries/singapore.json), GPL-3.0, with its complete license retained in `public/licenses/singapore-boundary-GPL-3.0.txt`. All source features are unioned and lightly simplified; the outline includes coastal waters, so polygon area is not land-only area. The derived combined `public/maps/international-regions.geojson` dataset is distributed under GPL-3.0; its editable GeoJSON source, transformation script (`scripts/build-international-regions.py`) and upstream source URLs are provided in this repository. This dataset license does not change the licenses of independent application code.
- Fuji municipal geometry (code 22210): [JapanCityGeoJson 2020](https://github.com/niiyz/JapanCityGeoJson/blob/master/geojson/22/22210.json), derived from Japan Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism [National Land Numerical Information, Administrative Zones N03](https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/gml/datalist/KsjTmplt-N03-v2_4.html). Reuse under the source's Government of Japan Standard Terms of Use 2.0 (compatible with CC BY 4.0): [terms](https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/other/agreement.html). GeoJSON unioned, simplified and measured for this website; not a government-endorsed or current cadastral survey.
